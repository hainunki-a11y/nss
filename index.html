About NSS TSEC

The NSS unit of Thakur Shyamnarayan Engineering College is driven by the philosophy of social service and personality development. Through regular camps, environmental drives, health awareness, and community outreach, we instill the spirit of volunteerism among engineering students. Our aim is to bridge the gap between the privileged and underprivileged sections of society while nurturing responsible citizens.
